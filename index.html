Welcome to the World of Technology

In this presentation, we will explore some of the most important modern technologies and how they have changed the way we learn, work and communicate.

01

📱 Smart Devices

Smart Technology

Smart devices are electronic devices that can perform different tasks, interact with users and connect to the Internet.

📱 Smartphones

Used for communication, learning, photography, applications and Internet access.

⌚ Smart Watches

Help users track activities, notifications and different health-related information.

🏠 Smart Home Devices

Examples include smart TVs, refrigerators and robotic vacuum cleaners.

⚡ Advantages

Speed, convenience, Internet connectivity and access to many services.

Developed by Ammar Al Haddar
02

☁️ Cloud Computing

Cloud Technology

Cloud computing is the use of Internet-based servers to store data, run applications and provide services instead of depending completely on the user's device.

💾 Storage

Stores files, photos and data on Internet-based servers.

🌐 Access

Users can access their files and services from different devices.

🔒 Security

Cloud services provide different systems to protect data and accounts.

👥 Collaboration

Allows users to share files and work together online.

Developed by Ammar Al Haddar
03

🥽 Virtual & Augmented Reality

VR & AR

Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality allow users to interact with digital environments or digital elements in new and interactive ways.

🥽 Virtual Reality

Creates a three-dimensional digital environment that users can interact with using special devices.

📷 Augmented Reality

Adds digital elements to the real world using phones or specialized devices.

🎮 Gaming

VR and AR are widely used in games and interactive experiences.

🎓 Education

These technologies can make learning more interactive through models and simulations.

Developed by Ammar Al Haddar
04
✦

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

AI

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human abilities.

🧠 Machine Learning

Helps systems learn patterns from data.

💬 Language Processing

Allows computers to understand and process human language.

👁️ Image Analysis

AI systems can analyze images and identify different elements.

🚀 Applications

Used in education, medicine, industry, programming and many other fields.

Developed by Ammar Al Haddar
05

🌐 Internet of Things

Internet of Things - IoT

The Internet of Things connects devices and objects to the Internet, allowing them to collect and exchange data and perform different functions.

🏠 Smart Homes

Allows users to control lights and household devices through the Internet.

🚗 Smart Cars

Modern cars can use connectivity and data to improve different services and functions.

🏭 Industry

Sensors and connected devices can monitor machines and industrial processes.

📊 Data

Connected devices collect data and send it to other systems for analysis.
